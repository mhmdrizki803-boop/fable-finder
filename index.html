Fable Finder

Type the lesson you keep forgetting and get a four line fable with a picture, written on the spot. One button reads it out loud.

Signing in is a plain OAuth + PKCE loop in your browser, and your own Pollen pays for the writing and the picture. Nothing is proxied.

Kept fables

What it actually does

  • A text model turns your lesson into a title, four short lines and a moral, and it also writes the one line prompt for the picture.
  • An image model draws that picture on the spot, so no two fables look alike.
  • The read aloud button sends the same words to a voice model, so the fable works with your eyes closed.
  • Kept fables live in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.
